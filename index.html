HOW TO PLAY

  • Aim with the mouse (or drag on a touch screen) and hold to fire into the arena.
  • Watch the scope. The ring around your sight is your hit radius, and the plate beside it names the target: LOCKED means a shot at that point connects, OFF TARGET means the body is under the sight but outside the ring.
  • Defend the core at the centre - it is always marked by a ground disc, a vertical light beacon and an expanding sonar halo, from any angle.
  • Draft an upgrade every time you clear a wave. Two maxed cards can fuse into an evolution.
  • Watch the floor. Every enemy carries a glowing ring on the ground - spawn rings appear before the enemy does, so scanning for rings finds targets fast.
  • Shoot the pods enemies drop - powerups are collected with a shot, not by touching them.
  • Defuse Void Mines before their fuse runs out; the blast also shoves enemies around.
  • Build combo to charge Hyper Mode: the world slows, you speed up.
  • Spend cores in the Arsenal and finish contracts to unlock weapons and arena skins.
  • Daily Challenge uses a UTC seed: everyone worldwide gets the same waves and the same drafts.

Keys: Esc pause · R restart · M mute · F3 debug overlay (with ?debug=1) · 1/2/3 pick a draft card
